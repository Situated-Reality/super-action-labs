# Brand guide

## Brand idea

Super-Action Labs builds intelligence that perceives context, negotiates intervention, and amplifies human action. The visual identity should feel precise, forward-moving, spatial, and restrained.

## Mark

The canonical vector asset is `assets/mark.svg`.

The geometry expresses four ideas:

- **Action** — parallel trajectories suggest continuous progress.
- **Intelligence** — the central circle represents the reasoning core.
- **Connection** — the elements form a coordinated system.
- **Forward** — the overall stance leans into the future.

Use the mark in black on light backgrounds or white on dark backgrounds. Preserve its aspect ratio, provide clear space, and do not rotate, outline, stretch, add effects, or rearrange its elements.

## Color

| Role | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Paper | `--paper` | `#f3f2ee` |
| Ink | `--ink` | `#101111` |
| White | `--white` | `#f7f6f2` |
| Muted text | `--muted` | `#7a7b78` |
| Brand accent | `--accent` | `#d9ff43` |
| Warning telemetry | — | `#ff9f43` |
| Critical telemetry | — | `#ff5b45` |

The yellow-green accent signals agency, live state, and forward action. Use it selectively for important words, active states, and high-confidence positive signals. Orange and red are semantic warning colors, not decorative alternatives.

## Typography

- **Manrope** — headlines, body copy, and product statements
- **DM Mono** — telemetry, indices, labels, controls, and technical metadata

Use large, light-weight headlines with tight tracking. Italics can introduce motion or conceptual contrast. Technical labels should be compact, uppercase, and letter-spaced, but never so small that readability is compromised.

## Voice

The writing should be:

- Direct, confident, and commercially legible
- Grounded in human benefit rather than model spectacle
- Technical enough to be credible without becoming academic shorthand
- Precise about what is demonstrated, simulated, researched, or available
- Restrained; avoid inflated claims such as “revolutionary,” “human-level,” or “fully autonomous”

Preferred framing:

- “Understand before acting.”
- “Help now, wait, ask, or step back.”
- “Context travels. Interfaces change.”
- “Amplify action. Preserve agency.”

## Imagery

Imagery should be monochrome or tightly desaturated, architectural, and editorial. Favor real environments, human–AI collaboration, spatial depth, and subtle technical overlays. Avoid generic neon robots, glowing brains, visual clutter, and consumer-stock imagery.

## Motion

Motion communicates state, causality, or spatial relationship. It should be calm and reversible:

- Hover and click interactions may shift, scan, pulse, or reveal.
- Live telemetry changes should ease between states.
- Movement should not obstruct reading or imply false precision.
- Always preserve a useful reduced-motion experience.

## Naming

Use **Super-Action Labs** in prose and **SUPER-ACTION LABS** in the compact wordmark treatment. The public domain is `super-action.org`; the canonical web address is `www.super-action.org`.
